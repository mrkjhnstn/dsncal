DSNDay

DSNDay is a small single-page HTML application that shows the current time (UTC, and local at each DSCC).
It also shows a calendar of 6 months indicating week of year, month/day, and day-of-year.

It opens in a separate smaller popup window and updates every minute.

If you close the popup, reload this page and click the button again.

If the popup does not open, check your popup settings and allow them from this site.
